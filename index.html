How this would work on our platform

  1. Wholesaler uploads 3–5 photos of the product from different angles (front, back, sides, top). One photo works, but more angles give a much cleaner model.
  2. AI converts the photos into a 3D model (GLB for Android/web, USDZ for iPhone) and a short 360° spin video. This would be a future integration with a photo-to-3D service such as Meshy, Tripo or Luma AI. Nothing is connected in this demo.
  3. Quick review: the wholesaler checks the 3D preview and approves it, or re-shoots if it looks wrong.
  4. Resellers import it with the product. The 3D model and spin video are just files attached to the product, so they come across with the title, price and photos when a reseller adds the product to their shop.
  5. Shoppers try before ordering: rotate it in 3D, place furniture life-size in their room with AR, or try sunglasses on with the front camera.

Works best for solid items (furniture, sunglasses, shoes, bags, lamps). Clothing try-on is harder: AI can show a garment on a model or photo, but it is a visual preview, not an exact fit.
